## Prompt

```text
Create an ultra-photorealistic fashion portrait of a naturally beautiful young adult woman wearing an extravagant colorful vintage-inspired outfit.

Character Appearance:
A young East Asian woman with delicate natural facial features, warm fair skin, soft brown almond-shaped eyes, naturally defined eyebrows, subtle freckles, a small beauty mark near the lower cheek, and naturally glossy muted pink lips. Her hair is dark brown, short-to-medium length, messy layered shag haircut with soft wispy bangs, natural volume and numerous loose flyaway strands.

Hair Details:
Beautifully messy, voluminous dark brown hair with individual strands illuminated by sunlight. Natural movement, imperfect texture and loose strands framing both sides of her face.

Outfit:
An oversized, richly patterned vintage bohemian jacket featuring vibrant orange, red, turquoise, emerald green, pink and cream colors. Intricate decorative motifs, embroidered-looking patterns, traditional textile-inspired prints and layered fabrics. Underneath, a colorful patterned top with a beige crossbody bag strap running diagonally across her chest.

Accessories:
Large statement ethnic-inspired dangling earrings featuring colorful beads, miniature decorative ornaments, tassels and intricate details.

Pose and Expression:
Medium close-up portrait from the waist upward. Her body is slightly turned while her face looks directly toward the camera. A calm, naturally expressive gaze with slightly parted lips. Relaxed shoulders and effortless candid posture.

Environment:
An outdoor urban setting with a softly blurred architectural background, natural greenery and subtle circular bokeh highlights. Background should remain unobtrusive and beautifully out of focus.

Lighting:
Bright natural daylight with beautiful backlighting illuminating her messy hair. Soft sunlight highlights across her face, realistic facial shadows, delicate light reflections and natural skin tones.

Photography Style:
Premium editorial fashion photography, authentic 85mm DSLR portrait, shallow depth of field, cinematic natural light, realistic optical bokeh, subtle film grain, lifelike colors, documentary-style candid composition.

Image Quality:
Ultra-photorealistic RAW DSLR photography, 8K ultra-high resolution, incredibly detailed skin pores, natural facial imperfections, individual eyelashes, realistic hair strands, intricate fabric patterns, detailed embroidery and authentic textile textures.

Negative Instructions:
No plastic skin, no excessive beauty filters, no artificial smoothness, no CGI, no illustration, no anime, no distorted hands, no unnatural facial proportions, no text, no typography, no watermark.

Vertical 3:4 portrait composition.
```
