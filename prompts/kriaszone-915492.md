## Prompt

```text
An authentic, natural-looking travel photograph taken on an island. A woman rests in the shade by the seaside, leaning casually against the trunk of a sturdy tree. One hand lightly touches the brim of her straw hat, which is stirred ever so slightly by the sea breeze; her posture is relaxed and at ease—the kind of moment captured spontaneously during a journey. The shot is framed from the side or back, avoiding a focus on facial features and leaving age, ethnicity, or specific identity ambiguous. The subject and the tree trunk are positioned in the mid-ground, with the canopy and tropical foliage above casting a soft, dappled shade. In the foreground, a clean, light-colored sandy beach with sparse natural vegetation is visible, while the calm sea and a clear horizon stretch out in the distance, creating an open, well-layered environment. The 3:4 vertical composition establishes a natural balance between the subject, the tree, and the island seascape, while maintaining ample surrounding space. Shot from roughly eye level, the medium-to-long-range framing ensures the island setting remains clearly discernible. Soft, natural daylight filters through the leaves, creating a delicate, dappled play of light and shadow; the subject rests in a bright, comfortable patch of shade, with skin tones and environmental exposure rendered naturally—neither overexposed nor overly dramatic. The color palette is fresh and harmonious, dominated by the soft blue-greens of the sea, the warm, light tones of the sand, and the natural green of the foliage, with the straw hat revealing its authentic woven texture. The overall atmosphere is one of relaxation, tranquility, and pleasant vacation vibes. It possesses the quality of a genuine travel snapshot—natural in its details and relaxed in its composition—free from excessive retouching, exaggerated filters, intense saturation, or the staged, artificial feel of a studio photoshoot.
```
