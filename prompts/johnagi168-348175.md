## Prompt

```text
A cinematic romantic still photograph captured on a 35mm motion picture camera — a slightly low-angle medium three-quarter shot of an extraordinarily attractive, movie-star-caliber young East Asian couple embracing on a tropical beach at golden-hour sunset, capturing the joyful moment as the man effortlessly lifts the woman high into the air against a pastel pink and gold twilight sky.

The strikingly handsome, tall, broad-shouldered man in his mid-20s possesses sculpted aristocratic bone structure, a sharp chiseled jawline, a high straight nose bridge, deep expressive eyes, and effortlessly styled tousled jet-black hair. Wearing an unbuttoned relaxed black linen shirt with rolled sleeves, his strong arms wrap securely around her thighs to lift her upward as he gazes up at her with a warm, captivating smile. The breathtakingly gorgeous, slender woman features an exquisite delicate side profile, graceful swan neck, luminous rosy-flushed porcelain skin, and a sculpted bare back. Wearing a lightweight white backless spaghetti-strap sundress tied with a large silk ribbon bow at her lower back, she rests her hands on his shoulders and looks down into his eyes with an enchanting, shy smile. Captured mid-motion, her lustrous dark chestnut hair and trailing white dress ribbons sweep dramatically through the sea breeze.

The setting is a serene tropical coastline with soft-focus palm tree silhouettes along the shore beneath a dreamy sky blending from twilight blue into warm pink and glowing sunset gold. Strong golden backlighting wraps her flying hair, bare back, and sheer flowing fabric in radiant warm rim light, while floating sun-kissed bokeh particles shimmer in the hazy sea air.

Captured with a wide-latitude cinema package and a 50mm vintage anamorphic lens at f/2.0, producing creamy oval bokeh, soft organic halation, and gentle lens flare. Flattering, fine, even real human skin with warm subsurface scattering, strand-by-strand windblown hair physics, natural linen and chiffon textures, and fine 35mm theatrical film grain — photographed on real film, never CGI, never plastic, never 3D render.
```
